Luros 1.0.2 speaks the language you choose and leaves the conversation alone while it writes.

- **Settings ▸ Preferences ▸ Language** chooses the interface language: follow the system, or any of the twelve the app carries, each named in itself. It writes the same preference iOS Settings ▸ Luros writes, so the two pages agree, and the choice opens with the next start.
- **A reply that streams in no longer drags the whole conversation with it.** A step's height is measured once and kept, the list is handed only the row a stream changed, and the viewport is left where it is while a turn writes. The line under the title says what the turn is doing now — the thought being written, or the tool it called.
- **The opening's caret sits on the right side in a right-to-left language**, at the line's start and after every letter written, instead of staying on the left as if every sentence ran left to right.
- **The camera, photo, file and web tiles** sit on the composer's own frosted glass rather than a 5% wash, so they stay legible over a picture.
- **The top bar** takes the page's colour instead of the system's white glaze, and stops where the navigation bar does.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later and an A12 chip or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
